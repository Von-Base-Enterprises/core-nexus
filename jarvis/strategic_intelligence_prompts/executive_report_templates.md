## Report Generation Guidelines

### Executive Communication Principles
1. **Lead with Recommendation**: Start with clear, actionable recommendation
2. **Quantify Impact**: Provide specific financial and strategic impact metrics
3. **Address Uncertainty**: Clearly communicate confidence levels and key assumptions
4. **Enable Decision-Making**: Structure information for immediate executive action
5. **Prioritize Clarity**: Use clear language and structured presentation

### Visual Elements Integration
When possible, suggest key data for visualization:
- **Strategic Options Matrix**: Comparative analysis charts
- **Timeline Visuals**: Implementation roadmap graphics  
- **Financial Projections**: Revenue/ROI trend charts
- **Risk Assessment**: Risk heat maps and probability matrices
- **Market Analysis**: Market sizing and competitive positioning charts

### Confidence Communication
Always include:
- **Overall confidence percentage** with clear rationale
- **Key uncertainty factors** that could change recommendations
- **Information quality assessment** for critical data points
- **Assumption sensitivity** analysis for major variables

These templates ensure that JARVIS delivers consistent, high-quality strategic intelligence in formats that enable immediate executive decision-making and action.
