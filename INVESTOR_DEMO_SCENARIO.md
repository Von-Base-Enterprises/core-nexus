**Show Real-Time Loading of Scattered Data**:

2. **Competitor Intelligence** (Web scraping simulation)

4. **Customer Feedback** (Unstructured text)

#### **Autonomous Analysis Phase** (2.5 minutes)
**Show Core Nexus Working**:

**Key Differentiator**: *"Notice Core Nexus isn't just retrieving similar documents - it's reasoning about relationships between market size, competition, and regulations to identify strategic patterns."*

**Autonomous Planning Features**:
- Multi-step reasoning chains

**Live Generation of**:
