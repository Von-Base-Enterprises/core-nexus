1. **Language Localization Critical**

   - French prospects: 100% require French language support

2. **Data Sovereignty Concerns**
   - 78% of enterprise prospects mentioned GDPR/data residency
   - Government entities require local data centers
   - Financial services have strictest requirements

3. **Competitive Displacement Opportunities**
